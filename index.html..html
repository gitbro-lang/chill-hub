About Chill Hub

A clean starting point for cloud play on phones, laptops, and Chromebooks.

Want gameplay embedded directly inside Chill Hub? That requires an approved provider integration, plus any required account credentials and domain approval. A normal website page cannot create cloud gaming servers by itself.

Tip: use a stable Wi‑Fi connection and check your school's device and network rules before gaming on a managed Chromebook.
